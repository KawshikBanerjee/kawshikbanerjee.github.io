Kawshik Banerjee

I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at Southern Illinois University Carbondale, advised by Dr. Khaled M. Saifuddin. My current research focuses on graph learning and multimodal learning.

I completed my undergrad in Computer Science and Engineering from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology, where I was advised by Azmain Yakin Srizon. My undergrad thesis focused on assessing how our understanding, usage, and the association of racial and ethnic stereotypical terms have shifted over more than a decade using social media data.

My research has previously been featured in Nature Index and Nature News.
